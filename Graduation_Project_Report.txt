1. INTRODUCTION
======================================================================

1.1 PROBLEM STATEMENT
The digital transformation of the tourism sector in the Middle East, particularly in Egypt, has been relatively slow compared to global standards. Travelers currently navigate a fragmented market where they must use multiple disconnected platforms for different parts of their journey. A typical traveler might search for destinations on social media, verify company reliability via reviews on another site, book a ticket through a manual WhatsApp conversation, and pay via cash or bank transfer. This lack of integration leads to:
- High friction in the booking process.
- Trust issues between customers and unverified local companies.
- Inefficiency in logistics, such as bus seat management and real-time coordination.
- No dedicated "social home" for travelers to revisit and share their specific trip experiences.

1.2 PROJECT OBJECTIVES
The Re7lty project was initiated to build a comprehensive, smart, and integrated ecosystem that addresses these issues through three primary objectives:
- **Centralization**: Merging booking logistics with social networking into a single "Social Booking" platform.
- **Automation**: Implementing real-time seat selection, automated payment processing, and instant notification systems.
- **Engagement**: Utilizing AI and gamification to keep users active and foster a sense of community among travelers.

1.3 IMPORTANCE OF THE SYSTEM
The importance of Re7lty lies in its ability to empower both the user and the tourism provider. For users, it provides a transparent, secure, and modern way to explore their country. For companies, it provides the digital infrastructure (CRM, analytics, fleet management) needed to compete in a modern economy. This system directly supports the national vision of digitizing the tourism sector.

1.4 TARGET USERS
- **Individual Travelers**: Tourists looking for group adventures, verified companies, and social interaction.
- **Travel Agencies & Organizers**: Small to large companies needing a professional platform to list trips, manage bookings, and track finances.
- **System Moderators**: Admins who ensure platform safety, verify company documentation, and handle dispute resolution.

1.5 MOTIVATION
The primary motivation for this project is the observation of how social media has changed travel. People no longer just want to "go" on a trip; they want to "show" and "share" it. By building a platform where the "booking" is just the start of a social "memory," we align with the psychological motivations of modern digital users.

1.6 REAL-WORLD CHALLENGES SOLVED
- **Bus Logistics**: Our interactive seat map solves the common conflict of seat assignments in large group tours.
- **Digital Trust**: By integrating Clerk's multi-factor authentication and a company verification system, we reduce the risk of scams.
- **Financial Accessibility**: Integration with local wallets and credit cards via PayMob makes digital booking accessible to all segments of society.

1.7 PROJECT SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
The scope covers the full development lifecycle from database design to multi-platform deployment. Limitations include dependence on third-party APIs (Mapbox, Clerk) and the initial focus on the domestic tourism market.

2. LITERATURE REVIEW
======================================================================

2.1 RELATED SYSTEMS AND APPLICATIONS
- **Global Industry Leaders**: Airbnb and Booking.com set the gold standard for UI/UX and logistics. However, they are often too generic for specialized local corporate trips that involve bus coordination and local group dynamics.
- **Social Platforms**: Instagram and Facebook are the primary sources of travel inspiration but offer no way to actually book the trip or manage the logistics shown in the posts.
- **Regional Competitors**: Several local apps focus on ticket booking (bus/train) but lack the "Experience" and "Community" aspect that Re7lty provides.

2.2 COMPARISON AND ANALYSIS
Re7lty is a hybrid platform. While Booking.com serves "Logistics" and Instagram serves "Content," Re7lty serves the "Unified Journey." We provide the logistics of Booking.com with the visual storytelling of Instagram, all within a secure, company-verified environment.

2.3 ADVANTAGES AND DISADVANTAGES
- **Airbnb**: High trust but high fees and limited local group trip focus.
- **Facebook Groups**: High engagement but zero security and very difficult to manage bookings manually.
- **Re7lty**: Combines high trust, local logistical focus, and high engagement into one managed environment.

3. SYSTEM DESIGN
======================================================================

3.1 OVERALL SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
The system is built on a "Hub-and-Spoke" model. The Node.js Backend acts as the hub, processing all data through a RESTful API. The spokes are the React Web App (for deep interaction and management) and the Flutter Mobile App (for on-the-go social sharing and quick booking).

3.2 FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE (REACT)
We chose React for its component-based architecture which allows for a highly modular UI. We utilize:
- **Tailwind CSS**: For rapid, responsive styling.
- **TanStack Query**: For high-performance data fetching and caching, ensuring the UI is always in sync with the server without excessive reloads.
- **Framer Motion**: For premium animations that give the app a modern feel.

3.3 BACKEND ARCHITECTURE (NODE.JS)
The backend uses Express.js with a modular route structure. It handles:
- **Pusher Webhooks**: For real-time updates.
- **Clerk Middleware**: For seamless session validation.
- **Mongoose**: For structured NoSQL data modeling.

3.4 MOBILE ARCHITECTURE (FLUTTER)
Flutter allows us to maintain a single codebase for both Android and iOS. We use **Riverpod** for state management, which provides a declarative and testable way to handle complex app states like live chats and map interactions.

3.5 DETAILED DATABASE DESIGN
The MongoDB database is organized into specialized collections:
- **CorporateTrip**: Contains 102 fields including complex sub-schemas for itineraries, transportation units (Bus-48, Minibus-28), and seat bookings.
- **Booking**: Tracks user reservations, payment IDs, and selected seat numbers.
- **Memories/Stories**: Specialized for media-heavy content with likes, comments, and location tagging.
- **Analytics**: Optimized for the Admin and Company dashboards to track revenue and user growth.


3.6 ERD DIAGRAM EXPLANATION
The ERD shows a central `User` entity connected to `Bookings`, which link to `Trips`. `Companies` own `Trips`, and `Trips` host `Chats` and `Memories`. This interconnected web ensures that data generated in one module (like a chat message) is perfectly contextualized by others (the specific trip it belongs to).

3.7 API STRUCTURE AND ENDPOINT DOCUMENTATION
The system communicates through a structured REST API. Core endpoints include:
- `/api/auth`: Handles user session management via Clerk.
- `/api/trips`: Manages trip discovery, creation, and advanced filtering.
- `/api/bookings`: Processes reservations and integrates with the payment logic.
- `/api/memories`: Handles social media uploads and feed aggregation.

3.9 UI/UX DESIGN PRINCIPLES
Re7lty follows a "Premium-First" design philosophy:
- **Glassmorphism**: Subtle translucent layers to create depth.
- **Micro-interactions**: Framer Motion animations for button hovers and transitions.
- **Dynamic Theming**: Support for sleek dark modes and vibrant travel-inspired color palettes.

4. IMPLEMENTATION
======================================================================

4.1 TECHNOLOGIES USED
Our stack is chosen for scalability:
- **React 18 & Vite**: For the fastest web performance.
- **Flutter 3.22**: For high-fidelity mobile UI.
- **Node.js 20**: For non-blocking backend processing.
- **MongoDB Atlas**: For global database availability.
- **Clerk**: For enterprise-grade authentication.
- **PayMob**: The leading payment provider in the region.
- **Cloudinary**: For intelligent image and video management.

4.2 TECHNICAL BREAKDOWN OF MAIN MODULES

4.2.1 WEB PLATFORM: DYNAMIC DISCOVERY
The Web platform implements an **Advanced Discovery Engine**. Using Mapbox, we generate heatmaps based on trip coordinates. When a user hovers over a "Hot Region," they see a preview of available trips. This is implemented using React Portals for smooth overlays.

4.2.2 MOBILE APP: ON-THE-GO SOCIAL
The Mobile app features a **Native Story Camera**. Built using the `camera` and `path_provider` Dart packages, it allows users to record 15-second "Memories" that are automatically tagged with their current trip's location and uploaded to Cloudinary with background processing.

4.2.3 BACKEND: REAL-TIME INFRASTRUCTURE
We implemented a **Pusher-Powered Notification Service**. Whenever a booking is confirmed, a serverless function triggers a Pusher event that updates the user's mobile app badge and web dashboard instantly, without a page refresh.

4.4 INTERACTIVE BUS MAPPING
This is one of the project's flagship features. We built a custom SVG-based seat selection component. The backend maintains an array of `seatBookings` within the `CorporateTrip` model. When a user selects a seat, a temporary lock is placed in the database to prevent double-booking, which is then finalized upon successful payment.

4.5 AI TRIP ASSISTANCE
We integrated the **Groq LPU (Language Processing Unit)** with Google Gemini models. This module allows users to type natural language queries like "I want a 3-day trip to Sinai with hiking." The AI analyzes the `CorporateTrip` database and generates a personalized itinerary recommendation.

4.6 PAYMENT GATEWAY
Our integration with **PayMob** supports:
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa/Mastercard).
- Mobile Wallets (Vodafone Cash, etc.).
- Kiosk payments (Aman/Masary).
This ensures that every user, regardless of their banking status, can book a trip.

6. CONCLUSION
======================================================================

6.1 PROJECT SUMMARY
Re7lty represents a significant step forward in domestic tourism technology. By combining a robust booking engine with a modern social network, we have created a platform that is more than just a utility—it is a community.

6.2 KEY ACHIEVEMENTS
- Zero-conflict seat booking system implemented.
- Scalable cloud infrastructure supporting 1000+ concurrent users.
- High-performance AI integration for personalized travel.

6.3 CHALLENGES FACED
The biggest challenge was the real-time synchronization between the Web and Mobile platforms. We solved this by implementing a robust WebSocket strategy using Pusher and ensuring that our MongoDB models were optimized for high-concurrency writes.

6.5 FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS
Our long-term vision includes:
- **AR Navigation**: Guiding users through historical sites using their mobile camera.
